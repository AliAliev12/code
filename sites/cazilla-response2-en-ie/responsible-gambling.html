Licensed operators must implement responsible gambling measures, verify player ages, and provide access to support resources.

Recognising Problem Gambling

Problem gambling can affect anyone, regardless of age, background, or income. Common warning signs include spending more than you can afford, chasing losses, gambling to escape stress or anxiety, lying to friends or family about gambling activity, and feeling unable to stop even when you want to. If any of these signs feel familiar, it is important to seek help as soon as possible.
