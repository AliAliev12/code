Recognising problem gambling

Gambling is meant to be entertainment, full stop. It's not a way to chase back losses or take the edge off stress. Some warning signs worth knowing: chasing losses, borrowing money to fund play, letting work or family slide, or feeling on edge when you're not gambling. If any of that sounds familiar — for yourself or someone close to you — the support tools below are there for a reason. Any good online casino ireland operator worth its salt will have these options clearly signposted in your account settings.

Safer play tools available to you

Licensed casinos operating in Ireland are expected to offer a solid range of player-protection features. You can request any of the following at any time — it's your right:

  • Deposit limits — set daily, weekly or monthly caps on how much you add to your account.
  • Loss limits — put a ceiling on the amount you can lose within a set period.
  • Session time limits — get an alert or be automatically logged out after a chosen stretch of play.
  • Reality checks — on-screen prompts that show how long you've been playing and where you stand financially.
  • Cool-off periods — step away for anywhere between 24 hours and 30 days without closing your account entirely.
  • Self-exclusion — block yourself from one site or several for a minimum of six months.

Self-exclusion is one of the strongest tools available. Once it's in place, the operator must close your account, return any balance remaining and stop you from reopening it during the exclusion window. To self-exclude across several Irish-licensed operators at once, get in touch with the responsible gambling team at your casino or look into a national self-exclusion scheme if one is available to you.
