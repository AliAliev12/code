Self-Exclusion Options

Self-exclusion lets you block access to a casino for a set period — from 24 hours up to a permanent ban. Most licensed operators provide this directly in your account settings under Responsible Gambling or Player Safety. Ireland's national self-exclusion register, operated through the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland (GRAI), allows you to exclude yourself from multiple operators at once. Once set, self-exclusion cannot be reversed during the chosen period.

Support Helplines in Ireland

If gambling is causing distress for you or someone close to you, free and confidential support is available:

  • Gambling Care Ireland — gamblingcare.ie
  • Problem Gambling Ireland — problemgambling.ie
  • Samaritans Ireland — Freephone 116 123, available 24 hours a day.
  • Gamblers Anonymous Ireland — peer support meetings nationwide; gamblersanonymous.ie

You do not need to be in crisis to reach out. Early conversations with a support service can prevent problems from escalating.
