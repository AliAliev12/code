Editorial Independence

Our editorial team operates without direction from casino operators, affiliate networks, or advertisers. Ratings, rankings, and written assessments reflect the genuine findings of our reviewers. No operator can purchase a higher score, request removal of a negative finding, or influence the wording of a published review. When a casino is featured on this site, that placement is based solely on editorial merit as assessed against our published methodology.

We do earn referral fees when a reader clicks through to an operator and registers. This commercial relationship is disclosed clearly on relevant pages. However, it does not alter scores, affect which platforms are covered, or change the conclusions we reach. Our editorial decisions and our commercial relationships are managed separately.

Review Methodology

Every assessment follows a consistent framework. We examine licensing status first — confirming whether a platform is online casino legal in ireland under the relevant regulatory framework before we proceed. We then evaluate game libraries, payment options, customer support responsiveness, bonus terms, and responsible gambling tools.

Bonus structures receive particular scrutiny. Scores are assigned on a 1–10 scale across weighted categories. The final rating is a composite; no single category can inflate an overall score if other areas fall short. Methodology is reviewed internally at least twice per year and updated when regulatory or market conditions change.

Conflicts of Interest

We maintain a register of commercial relationships. Any reviewer who has a personal, financial, or professional connection to an operator under review must declare that conflict before the assignment is allocated. Where a conflict cannot be managed through reassignment, the review is either deferred or clearly labelled with the nature of the relationship.

Staff are prohibited from holding accounts with operators they are actively reviewing. Complimentary bonuses or promotional credits offered by operators are declined during the review period. These policies apply equally to freelance contributors working under contract for this site.
