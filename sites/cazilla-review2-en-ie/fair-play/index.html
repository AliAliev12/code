Editorial Independence

Our editorial team takes no direction from casino operators, software providers, or affiliate networks. No operator we review has any say in the scores, ratings, or written assessments we produce. Where commercial relationships exist, we disclose them plainly — and they play no part in shaping what we actually write. No commercial consideration changes that.

Our testing process is structured and consistent.
