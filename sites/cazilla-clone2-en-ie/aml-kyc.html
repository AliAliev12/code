What AML obligations mean for players in Ireland

Anti-Money Laundering rules require licensed operators to monitor transactions, flag unusual activity, and report suspicious behaviour to the relevant authorities. As a newest online casino ireland entrant or a returning player, you may notice that certain deposit thresholds or withdrawal requests trigger a compliance review. Identity verification and KYC checks explained

KYC (Know Your Customer) is the process by which a casino confirms who you are before allowing significant financial activity. You will typically be asked to submit a government-issued photo ID, proof of address dated within the last three months, and in some cases a copy of your payment method. Verification is usually completed within 24 to 72 hours. Until your account is verified, certain features — including higher withdrawals — may be restricted. Source of funds and enhanced due diligence

Beyond basic identity checks, operators may request source of funds documentation when deposits reach regulatory thresholds or when patterns appear inconsistent with a player's stated profile. Acceptable evidence includes recent payslips, bank statements, or proof of a business income. This enhanced due diligence step is standard across all regulated markets and is not a reflection on individual players.
