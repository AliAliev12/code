Helplines and Support Organisations

If you or someone you know is struggling with problem gambling, free and confidential help is available in Ireland right now. The following organisations offer support:

  • Gambling Care Ireland — gamblingcare.ie — free counselling and support services nationwide.
  • Gamblers Anonymous Ireland — ga.ie — peer support meetings and a 24-hour helpline.
  • Problem Gambling Ireland — problemgambling.ie — information, self-assessment tools, and referral services.
  • Samaritans Ireland — 116 123 (free, 24/7) — emotional support for anyone in distress.

You do not need to be in crisis to reach out. Early conversations with these services can prevent gambling from escalating into a serious problem.
