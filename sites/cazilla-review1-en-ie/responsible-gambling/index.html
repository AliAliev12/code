Why responsible gambling matters for Irish players

Problem gambling can affect anyone, regardless of income, experience, or how casually a person started playing. Recognising the early signs is the most effective form of protection available.

Common warning signs include spending more than you planned, chasing losses after a bad session, feeling anxious or irritable when not playing, and borrowing money to fund gambling activity. Practical tools to manage your play

Responsible gambling is not about avoiding fun — it is about staying in control. Most reputable platforms offer a range of self-management tools you can activate directly from your account settings. These typically include daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limits; session duration alerts; cooling-off periods; and full self-exclusion if you feel you need a longer break. We recommend setting deposit limits before you start playing, not after a difficult session when emotions may affect your judgement.
