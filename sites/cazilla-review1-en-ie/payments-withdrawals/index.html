Withdrawal Processes and Timescales

Withdrawals at licensed casinos follow a verification process known as KYC (Know Your Customer). Before your first cashout is approved, you will typically need to submit proof of identity and proof of address. This is a regulatory requirement, not an optional step, and completing it promptly avoids delays. Once verified, most e-wallet withdrawals including PayPal are processed within 24 hours, while bank transfers may take three to five business days depending on the operator and your bank.
