Cookies, data consent and your privacy rights

Every time you land on an online casino that legally operates in Ireland, the operator is obliged under the ePrivacy Regulations and GDPR — as they apply in Irish law — to seek your explicit consent before dropping any non-essential cookies onto your device. These typically include analytics trackers, personalisation tools, and advertising pixels. A properly compliant casino will show a clear consent banner on your first visit, offer a genuine choice over each cookie category, and remember your preference for a set period — generally no more than twelve months before prompting you again. Crucially, if you decline non-essential cookies, the site must still carry out its core functions: logging you in, handling payments, and keeping your session secure.

As a player based in the Republic of Ireland, you hold a meaningful set of data rights — including the right to access, correct, and delete personal information held about you, along with the right to data portability. Reputable operators publish a comprehensive privacy policy that clearly names the data controller, lists every third party your information is shared with, and explains how long records are kept for anti-money-laundering and KYC purposes. Before you register anywhere, it is worth taking a few minutes to check that the casino's cookie policy and privacy notice are written in plain English, have been updated within the past year, and specifically reference the Irish Data Protection Commission as the supervisory authority.
