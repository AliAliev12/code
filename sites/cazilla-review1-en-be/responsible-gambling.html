Responsible Gambling: Play Safely at Cazilla

A practical guide to safer gambling tools, self-exclusion options, and support services for players in Belgium.

This page is put together by an independent editorial team focused on helping Belgian players make genuinely informed choices. Whether you play free online casino games for fun or stake real money, responsible gambling has to come first — full stop. Nothing here constitutes legal advice, but it does reflect a real commitment to player welfare across every title and platform covered in our online casino reviews.

Why Age Verification Matters

Online gambling in Belgium is strictly limited to adults aged 18 and over. Licensed operators are legally obliged to confirm your age before you can access any real-money product — no exceptions. That covers popular online casino games like slots, roulette, blackjack, and live dealer tables, and it applies just as much to newer platforms such as an online crypto casino that accepts digital currencies. If you are under 18, please leave any gambling site straight away. Parents and guardians are strongly advised to install parental control software to block underage access at the device level.

Self-Exclusion Tools Available in Belgium

Belgian law gives players access to a national self-exclusion register called EPIS (Excluded Persons Information System), run by the Belgian Gaming Commission. Once you register, every licensed Belgian operator is legally required to block your account — it is not optional on their end. You can apply for a minimum exclusion period of three months or go for a permanent ban if that is what you need. On top of EPIS, most individual operators offer their own cooling-off periods and account-level exclusion settings directly in your profile. If gambling is starting to feel out of control, use these tools now rather than later. The process is free, confidential, and fully enforceable against any licensed site.

Safer Play Features You Should Use

Any operator worth recommending will give you a solid set of tools to keep things manageable. Deposit limits, loss limits, session timers, reality checks, and the option to take a short break are all worth setting up. The key is to put those limits in place before you start playing — not after a rough session when emotions are running high. Check your account history from time to time, and treat every gambling session, free-play modes included, as entertainment with a clear budget attached. Our editorial team only highlights platforms where these features are straightforward to find and activate, not buried in a settings menu somewhere.

Helplines and Support Organisations

If you or someone close to you needs to talk to someone, the organisations below offer confidential support in Belgium:

Gamblers Anonymous Belgium runs regular meetings and can be contacted through their national website. Gokken op Maat / Jouer Juste is a specialist counselling service for gambling-related difficulties, reachable by phone and online chat. The Centre de Référence en Santé Mentale (CRéSaM) and regional mental health centres also offer professional guidance. Do not wait for things to reach a crisis point — getting in touch early genuinely makes a difference.
