Responsible gambling at Cazilla

Everything you need to know about playing safely in Belgium — from self-exclusion tools to helplines and your rights as a player.

This is an independent editorial site, and we think the best players are well-informed ones. Some people play free online casino games simply to try out titles before putting real money on the line. Others enjoy regular real-money sessions. Either way, knowing your limits — and the tools that exist to help you stick to them — genuinely matters. Below you'll find a practical overview of responsible gambling in Belgium: age rules, self-exclusion options, support contacts, and what licensed operators are actually required to offer you.

Age verification and who can play

Online gambling in Belgium falls under the oversight of the Belgian Gaming Commission (BGC). The legal minimum age is 21 years old — no exceptions. Anyone under 21 cannot open a real-money account or access gambling products on a licensed platform. Decent operators check your age when you register and again when you request a withdrawal. If you've landed on our online casino reviews and you're not yet 21, please don't try to sign up anywhere. That applies across the board — popular online casino games like slots, roulette, blackjack, and live dealer tables, as well as any online crypto casino serving Belgian residents.

Self-exclusion and limit-setting tools

Belgian law obliges licensed operators to provide real self-exclusion options, not just a buried checkbox. The national register, EPIS, lets you block yourself from every BGC-licensed gambling site in one go. You can sign up through the official EPIS portal or ask a licensed casino's responsible gambling team to handle it for you. Exclusion periods start at three months and can go all the way to a permanent ban. Beyond EPIS, most licensed platforms let you set deposit limits, loss limits, session time caps, and reality-check reminders. Our honest advice: use these tools before you feel you need them, not after. When our editorial team writes any review on this site, we specifically check whether these features are easy to find and clearly explained.

Helplines and support organisations

If gambling is causing you or someone close to you real stress, help is out there — free and confidential. The main helpline is Gokinfo / Joueurs Anonymes, available on 0800 35 777 at no cost, in both Dutch and French. Gamblers Anonymous Belgium runs peer-support groups in various locations around the country. And if you need broader mental health support linked to gambling harm, Télé-Accueil (106) is available around the clock. You don't have to be in crisis to pick up the phone — talking early is often what stops things from getting worse.
