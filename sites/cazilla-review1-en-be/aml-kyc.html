Why AML and KYC Matter for Online Casino Players

In Belgium, licensed online casinos are legally required to have solid AML frameworks in place. These rules come from both EU directives and Belgian national legislation, enforced by the Gaming Commission. The purpose is simple: stop the platform from being used to launder dirty money or fund criminal activity. In practice, this means that before you can deposit, enjoy the best online casino games, or cash out your winnings, the casino needs to confirm your identity and the origin of your funds. Source of Funds and Enhanced Due Diligence

On top of the standard identity checks, Cazilla's AML policy means the platform also needs to look at where your money comes from in certain situations.
