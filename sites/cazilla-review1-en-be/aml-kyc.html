What is KYC and why does it matter?

Know Your Customer — KYC — is a mandatory process that every licensed gambling operator must complete to confirm who their players actually are. When you open an account with Cazilla, you will need to submit documents proving your identity. That typically means a government-issued photo ID, such as a national identity card or passport, plus proof of your home address, like a recent utility bill or bank statement. These checks are not optional. They are required under Belgian and European regulatory frameworks put in place to prevent fraud and protect people who may be at risk.

In most cases, identity checks need to be completed before your first withdrawal is processed, and sometimes before you reach certain deposit thresholds. Submitting accurate documents from the start keeps things moving and avoids unnecessary delays when you want to access your winnings.

Anti-money laundering obligations explained

AML — Anti-Money Laundering — rules require online gambling operators to keep a close eye on transactions and flag anything that looks suspicious. Like every regulated platform, Cazilla is legally obliged to report unusual activity to the relevant financial intelligence authorities. That could mean unusually large deposits, funds moving in and out very quickly, or patterns that simply do not match a player's stated profile.

As part of these obligations, Cazilla may ask for a source of funds declaration. In plain terms, you might be asked to show where the money you are depositing actually comes from — payslips, bank statements, or documentation of a business transaction, for example. It can feel like a lot to ask, but it is a legal requirement that protects both the platform and the broader financial system. Players who are unable to provide satisfactory documentation may find their accounts suspended while the matter is reviewed further.

Verification steps and timelines

Cazilla has set up its verification process to be as clear and manageable as possible. Once you have submitted your documents, the compliance team generally reviews them within 24 to 72 hours. You will get a notification as soon as your account is fully verified. Some features may be restricted in the meantime, so it is a good idea to complete verification early rather than waiting until a withdrawal request kicks the process off.

If you regularly play best online casino games and your spending reaches higher levels, enhanced due diligence may apply. This is standard practice across the regulated industry and reflects the operator's broader duty of care towards its players.
