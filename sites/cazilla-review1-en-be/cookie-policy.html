What cookies are and why we use them

A cookie is essentially a small text file that a website places on your device when you visit. On this site, cookies serve a few practical purposes: remembering your preferences, tracking how different pages get used, and making sure age-verification and consent choices stick between sessions. For instance, once you confirm you're at least 21 — the minimum age for gambling content in Belgium — that confirmation gets stored in a cookie so you won't be prompted again during the same session. Consent categories and what each one covers

We split cookies into four groups. Strictly necessary cookies keep things running: they handle your age-gate response, log your consent choices, and take care of basic security.
