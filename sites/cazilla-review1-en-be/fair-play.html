How we keep Cazilla reviews honest, independent, and free from commercial influence.

This page sets out the principles behind everything we publish. We cover editorial independence, how we actually test and score platforms, what happens when something needs correcting, and how we handle conflicts of interest. Any questions? The contact details are in the footer.

Editorial independence

We run an independent editorial site. No operator, software provider, or affiliate network gets a say in what we write or how we score anything. Every online roulette casino game offering and every broader platform is judged on its own merits, using the same fixed criteria we apply across all our reviews. Cazilla is no different — being prominently featured here doesn't earn any brand special treatment.

We don't take money in exchange for better ratings, inflated scores, or burying negative findings. Where a commercial relationship does exist — say, an affiliate arrangement that earns us revenue when a reader clicks through to an operator — we say so clearly on the relevant page. That revenue keeps the site running, but it plays no part in our editorial conclusions.

Review methodology

Our reviewers test platforms hands-on. That means creating real accounts and, where practical, actually making deposits and withdrawals. We look at the range of best casino games online on offer, how the interface holds up in practice, licensing and regulatory standing, how quickly customer support responds, and whether bonus terms are genuinely fair. Every criterion carries a defined weight in the final score — and that weighting doesn't shift depending on the operator.

When we're working out which platform is the best online casino to play for a particular type of reader, we factor in things that matter specifically in Belgium: language support, available payment methods, and whether the operator complies with Belgian gaming regulations. We don't recommend unlicensed operators to Belgian residents, full stop.

Game-specific testing — including live dealer tables and the roulette game online casino variants on offer — is carried out by reviewers who document their sessions and cross-reference what they find with publicly available return-to-player data, where the operator or its certified game suppliers have disclosed it.

Corrections policy

We take factual accuracy seriously. If a reader spots an error — a wrong figure, outdated information, or a misrepresentation of an operator's terms — we look into it straight away. Confirmed errors get corrected within a reasonable timeframe, and we add a brief note to the relevant section to flag that a change was made. We don't quietly delete inaccurate content. Being open about our mistakes is part of what we owe our readers.
