Why AML and KYC Rules Exist

Online gambling operators licensed to take players from Ireland must put solid anti-money laundering controls in place under both Irish and EU law. The purpose is straightforward: keep criminal funds out of gambling platforms and protect the people using them. The newest online casino ireland launches face exactly the same requirements as operators who have been around for years — there's no grace period based on how recently a site went live. Compliance isn't a choice either; regulators can suspend or pull a licence entirely when these obligations aren't met.

Identity Verification: What You Will Be Asked to Provide

Once you register or hit certain activity levels, the casino will ask you to confirm who you are. Typically that means a government-issued photo ID — a passport or driving licence — proof of address from the last three months, and sometimes a selfie or short video verification step. These checks apply no matter how you deposit or what your balance looks like. If you're drawn to an online casino no minimum deposit setup, it's worth knowing that KYC is triggered by account activity and regulatory thresholds, not just by how much you put in.

But once cumulative deposits or withdrawals pass certain limits, enhanced due diligence comes into play.
