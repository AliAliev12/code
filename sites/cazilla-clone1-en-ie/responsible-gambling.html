Who Can Play — Age Verification and the 18+ Rule

In Ireland, the legal minimum age for gambling is 18 years. Every regulated operator is required to verify the age of all customers before allowing real-money play. If you are under 18, you must not attempt to register or deposit at any gambling site. Operators holding a valid online casino license ireland are legally obliged to carry out robust Know Your Customer checks, and accounts found to belong to minors are closed immediately with funds returned to the payment source. Parents and guardians are encouraged to use parental control software such as Gamban or Net Nanny to prevent underage access.

Safer Play Tools You Should Know About

Responsible operators provide a range of practical tools designed to keep gambling within safe boundaries. Deposit limits allow you to cap how much you add to your account each day, week, or month. Loss limits work similarly, capping the amount you can lose over a chosen period. Session time reminders and reality checks alert you when you have been playing for a set length of time, helping you stay aware of how long you have been logged in. Wagering limits restrict the total amount staked in any given period. Cool-off periods let you take a short break — typically 24 hours to 30 days — without fully closing your account. Self-Exclusion Options in Ireland

Self-exclusion is the most powerful tool available to players who feel their gambling is becoming problematic. You can request self-exclusion directly with an individual operator, typically for a minimum period of six months and up to five years or indefinitely. Ireland also participates in multi-operator self-exclusion schemes, meaning a single request can be applied across multiple licensed sites simultaneously. Once self-exclusion is active, the operator is obliged to close your account, return any remaining balance, and prevent you from reopening an account during the exclusion period. Marketing communications must also cease immediately. If you believe you have been contacted by a site after self-excluding, you should report this to the relevant licensing authority.
