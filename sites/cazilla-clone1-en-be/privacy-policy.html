GDPR and Belgian legal framework

This site follows the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as it applies in Belgium, along with the Belgian Act of 30 July 2018 on the protection of natural persons in relation to personal data processing. The legal grounds we rely on are legitimate interest for site analytics, and consent for cookies and contact forms. We do not sell, rent, or pass your personal data to third parties for marketing. When our editorial team looks at an online casino app or puts platforms side by side, no reader data is shared with those operators — full stop.

International data transfers

Our hosting setup may use servers based outside Belgium. Where that's the case, any data transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, which ensures your data stays protected to an equivalent standard. When we write about an international casino online, we always draw a clear line between that operator's own data practices and what we do here on our editorial site — the two are completely separate. Likewise, any content covering an international online casino brand reflects our own independent editorial view and has nothing to do with any data-sharing arrangement with that brand.
