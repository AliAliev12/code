Fair Play: Our Editorial Standards

How Cazilla keeps its reviews honest, independent, and accountable for readers in Belgium.

This Fair Play page sets out how our editorial team works, how we deal with potential conflicts of interest, and what happens when something needs to be corrected. Every piece of content published here — whether it covers an online roulette casino game, a slot title, or a live dealer product — is written without commercial pressure. Belgian readers deserve a straight answer about how we reach our conclusions, so here it is.

Editorial independence

Our editorial team works completely independently from casino operators, software providers, and affiliate networks. No operator can pay to change a review outcome, adjust a rating, or bury a negative finding. When we assess the best casino games online, we apply the same fixed criteria to every title, regardless of who is behind it. Yes, our revenue comes partly from referral arrangements — but those are disclosed openly and have no bearing on what we write. The editorial desk has the final say on everything published, and no commercial partner gets to read or veto copy before it goes live.

Our review methodology

Every review follows a documented, repeatable process. We look at game fairness, return-to-player figures, licensing validity, payment reliability, and customer support quality. When we name the best online casino to play, that verdict comes from a weighted scorecard covering all of those areas equally. For game-specific content — say, a roulette game online casino evaluation — we also check variant availability, table limits, live stream quality where relevant, and how transparent the RNG certification actually is. Scores are never rounded up to do an operator a favour.

Conflicts of interest

Any reviewer with a financial stake in an operator being assessed must flag that before the assignment is handed out. If reassignment cannot resolve the conflict, the review is either put on hold or published with a clear note explaining the relationship. Staff cannot accept gifts, free credits, or hospitality from operators beyond a small threshold used purely for product testing. Affiliate commission rates do not change based on review scores, and operators are told this upfront before any partnership gets started.

Corrections policy

We correct factual errors quickly and openly. If you spot an inaccuracy — an outdated licence number, a wrong minimum deposit figure, a misquoted bonus term — you can reach our editorial team directly using the contact address at the bottom of this page. Once a correction is verified, it goes live within five working days. A correction note is added to the relevant part of the article so that anyone who read the original version knows what changed. We do not delete errors silently or quietly overwrite them.
