Age restrictions and legal requirements in Belgium

Gambling in Belgium falls under the oversight of the Belgian Gaming Commission. The minimum legal age for any form of licensed gambling — including popular online casino games — is 21. That rule covers both physical venues and online platforms without exception. Nobody under 21 is permitted to open an account, make a deposit, or play for real money on any licensed Belgian site. Operators are legally obliged to check your age when you register. If you are under 21, please leave any gambling site right away. On our own site, an age verification gate appears on every page in line with this requirement.

Self-exclusion and account limits

Belgian players can register with the national self-exclusion system run by the Belgian Gaming Commission. Signing up to EPIS (Excluded Persons Information System) cuts off your access to every licensed gambling operator in the country — including those that offer an online crypto casino option. The minimum exclusion period is three years, and it is enforceable across all licensed platforms. Beyond the national register, most operators also let you set voluntary deposit limits, session time caps, loss limits, and cooling-off periods. We'd encourage you to use these tools before any issue arises, not just after one does. Something as simple as setting a weekly deposit limit before your first session can go a long way toward keeping gambling enjoyable and affordable.

Helplines and support organisations

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone close to you, confidential help is within reach in Belgium. The organisations below can all be contacted free of charge:

Gamblers Anonymous Belgium — peer support groups across the country; visit gamblers-anonymous.be.
Télé-Accueil / Tele-Onthaal — free 24/7 listening service; call 106 (French) or 107 (Dutch).
Aide Info Drogues — free helpline covering gambling addiction; call 0800 10 800 (free, confidential).
VAD (Vereniging voor Alcohol- en andere Drugproblemen) — information and referral for Dutch-speaking players; visit vad.be.

All of these services are free and available in Dutch, French, and German — Belgium's three official languages.
